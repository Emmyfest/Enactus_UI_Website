Privacy policy

How Enactus University of Ibadan handles personal data

Draft template. Have it reviewed against current Nigerian data protection law (including the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023) before launch, then remove this line.

What we collect

Only what the platform needs: name and email for accounts; membership details you give us (faculty, department, level, phone, matriculation number); recruitment applications; event registrations and attendance; training progress; and records of actions taken on the platform for security and accountability.

Why we use it

To run membership and recruitment, organise projects, events and training, report our impact, and keep the organisation accountable. We do not sell personal data.

What is public

Nothing about you is public unless you chose it or your role requires it. Matriculation numbers, phone numbers and private email addresses are never shown publicly. Alumni choose which profile fields appear, and profiles are reviewed before publication.

Who can see it

Only people whose role requires it, such as reviewers of applications. Access is limited by permissions, and important actions are logged.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, or to complain. Write to {{ contact_email }}. We will respond within a reasonable time and tell you if a legal or accountability record must be kept.

How long we keep it

Application data is kept for the recruitment cycle and a reasonable period after. Membership records are kept while you are a member and then archived. Records that prove how decisions were made (approvals, verified impact figures, financial records) are kept as institutional records. Accounts can be deactivated or anonymised on request.

Security

Passwords are hashed, connections use HTTPS, sign-in attempts are rate-limited, uploads are restricted, and private documents are only served after an access check.
